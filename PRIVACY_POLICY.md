# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 6, 2026

Privacy Extension For WhatsApp™ Web ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our browser extension.

## 1. Single Purpose

The sole purpose of Privacy Extension For WhatsApp™ Web is to provide screen privacy features (such as blurring text, names, profile pictures, and media elements) on the WhatsApp Web interface to protect users from shoulder-surfing in public or professional environments.

## 2. No Data Collection (100% Local Processing)

- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, tracking identifiers, or analytics data.
- **No Chat Content Tracking:** The extension operates entirely within your local browser ecosystem. It cannot read, log, or export your messages, contact lists, or account details to any external servers.
- **No Telemetry:** There are no third-party tracking scripts, analytics modules, or telemetry tools embedded inside the extension software.

## 3. Data Storage & Permissions

- **Storage Permission:** Any configurations you make (such as active blur options and preferences) are stored locally on your device via the `chrome.storage` API. This data never leaves your browser.
- **Host Permissions:** The extension requests host permissions for `https://web.whatsapp.com/*` exclusively to inject the local CSS and layout scripts required to blur your UI elements on hover.

## 4. Open Source & Transparency

This extension is an open-source project built on the original design by Lukas Lenhardt, now maintained by M Aryo Muzakki, and released under the MIT License. Our full codebase is completely transparent and publicly viewable for auditing on our official repository.

## 5. Third-Party Disclaimer

This extension is an independent, community-driven open-source project and has no official relationship, affiliation, or endorsement from WhatsApp Inc. or Meta Platforms, Inc.
